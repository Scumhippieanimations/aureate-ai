Prompt Guides

Master the art of prompting for cinematic, professional-grade results.

Image
The Cinematography Formula
Subject + setting + lighting + lens + mood + era. Example: "A lone detective, rain-soaked Shinjuku alley, neon backlight, 35mm wide angle, noir melancholy, 1970s film grain"
Voice
Voice Pacing Tips
Slow rate for dramatic narration. High pitch for younger characters. Always use complete sentences for natural rhythm — never fragments.
Scene
World Layering
Start macro (continent → city → district) then go micro (street → building → room → surface). Each layer adds believability to your world.
CGI
The Architecture Trick
Reference real architectural movements (Brutalism, Deconstructivism, Metabolism) for instantly coherent CGI world aesthetics. These design languages took decades to develop — borrow them freely.
