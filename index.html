Prompt Guides

Master the art of prompting for cinematic results.

Exodus
The Cinematography Formula
Subject + Setting + Lighting + Lens + Mood + Era. Example: "A lone detective, rain-soaked alley, neon backlight, 35mm wide angle, noir melancholy, 1940s film grain"
Chronicles
World Layering Technique
Start macro (a city) then add layers (rain, 2am, neon signs, one figure). Each detail adds believability and depth to the generated world.
Voice
Pacing for Trailers
Epic Narrator preset works best. Write full punctuated sentences. Ellipses create dramatic pauses... Exclamation marks add power! Short sentences hit harder.
Esus
AI Draw Prompts
Describe art style alongside subject: "Studio Ghibli style background painting, misty mountain village at dusk, warm light in windows"
